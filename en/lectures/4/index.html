Lecture 4

Duality: cups, caps and quantum dimension

What this lecture does

A particle has an antiparticle, and a pair can be created from the vacuum and annihilated back into it. In a monoidal category this is a dual: an object X∗X^{*}X∗ with a cup 1→X⊗X∗1\to X\otimes X^{*}1→X⊗X∗ and a cap X∗⊗X→1X^{*}\otimes X\to1X∗⊗X→1 that cancel in a zigzag. This lecture gives the axioms, proves that they determine the dual, bends wires with them (the adjunction of Lecture 2, made literal), and closes loops to get traces and quantum dimensions.

4.5 Dagger

Theorem 4.10 (Hilbfd\Hilb_{\mathrm{fd}}Hilbfd​ is dagger pivotal sketched). With X∗X^{*}X∗ given the inner product for which dual bases of orthonormal bases are orthonormal, the caps of Hilbfd\Hilb_{\mathrm{fd}}Hilbfd​ are the daggers of its cups: ev⁡~X=coev⁡X†\widetilde{\ev}_X=\coev_X^{\dagger}evX​=coevX†​ and ev⁡X=coev⁡~X†\ev_X=\widetilde{\coev}_X^{\dagger}evX​=coevX†​ (Heunen–Vicary Def. 3.51 and Prop. 3.52).

In an orthonormal basis, coev⁡†(ej⊗ek)=⟨∑iei⊗ei,ej⊗ek⟩=δjk.\begin{aligned} &\coev^{\dagger}(e_j\otimes e^{k})\\ &\quad=\Bigl\langle\sum_i e_i\otimes e^{i},e_j\otimes e^{k}\Bigr\rangle\\ &\quad=\delta_{jk}. \end{aligned}​coev†(ej​⊗ek)=⟨i∑​ei​⊗ei,ej​⊗ek⟩=δjk​.​ Spin-12\tfrac1221​ does not contradict it: there the cup is Ω\OmegaΩ, which identifies V∗V^{*}V∗ with VVV by an antisymmetric form, not by the inner product.

Summary

  • Handed on: cups and caps with straight snakes; duals are unique up to a unique isomorphism, and f∗f^{*}f∗ is the rotated box (Theorem 4.2).
  • Bending: Hom⁡(U⊗V,W)≅Hom⁡(U,W⊗V∗)\Hom(U\otimes V,W)\cong\Hom(U,W\otimes V^{*})Hom(U⊗V,W)≅Hom(U,W⊗V∗) (Theorem 4.5); teleportation is a snake.
  • Loops: a pivotal structure closes loops; traces are cyclic and multiplicative, and the quantum dimension dXd_XdX​ is a loop (Theorem 4.7).
  • The sign: the straight Penrose cap is −Ω†-\Omega^{\dagger}−Ω†, with loop −2-2−2 (Theorem 4.8); the canonical pivotal structure has κ1/2=−1\kappa_{1/2}=-1κ1/2​=−1.

2 Let XXX and YYY have left duals X∗X^{*}X∗ and Y∗Y^{*}Y∗ in a monoidal category. Which object, with a nested cup and cap, is a left dual of X⊗YX\otimes YX⊗Y?

The nested cup is 1→X⊗Y⊗Y∗⊗X∗1\to X\otimes Y\otimes Y^{*}\otimes X^{*}1→X⊗Y⊗Y∗⊗X∗: the inner cup for YYY sits between the legs of the outer one for XXX, and each snake for X⊗YX\otimes YX⊗Y is a snake for YYY inside a snake for XXX.
