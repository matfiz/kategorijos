Lecture 11

Algebras in tensor categories and module categories

What this lecture does

A fusion category acts on a category by functors X⊗−X\otimes-X⊗−, one for each object XXX, with coherent associativity and unit isomorphisms. A category with such an action is a module category. This lecture builds module categories from algebras and computes a rank-one module associator. Ostrik’s theorem says that every finite semisimple indecomposable module category comes from an algebra.

A module category is not a category of modules: its objects need not be modules over anything. Ostrik’s theorem (§11.3) relates the two.

Theorem 11.10 (Ostrik cited). Let C\cat{C}C be a fusion category over C\CC and M\cat{M}M a semisimple indecomposable C\cat{C}C-module category with finitely many simple objects. Summary

  • Handed on: an algebra in C\cat{C}C is (A,μ,ι)(A,\mu,\iota)(A,μ,ι); its right modules form a left C\cat{C}C-module category ModC(A)\mathrm{Mod}_{\cat{C}}(A)ModC​(A) (Definitions 11.1 and 11.3 and Theorem 11.5).
  • The module associator: a rank-one module over the part of VecGω\VecG[\omega]VecGω​ on HHH is a normalised ψ\psiψ with ψ(g,h)ψ(gh,k)=ω(g,h,k)ψ(h,k)ψ(g,hk)\psi(g,h)\psi(gh,k)=\omega(g,h,k)\psi(h,k)\psi(g,hk)ψ(g,h)ψ(gh,k)=ω(g,h,k)ψ(h,k)ψ(g,hk), which exists exactly when ω∣H\omega|_Hω∣H​ is a coboundary (Theorem 11.6).
  • Ostrik: in the semisimple case [M,N]=⨁cHom⁡(c⊗M,N)⊗c[M,N]=\bigoplus_c\Hom(c\otimes M,N)\otimes c[M,N]=⨁c​Hom(c⊗M,N)⊗c, and every finite semisimple indecomposable module category is Mod([M,M])\mathrm{Mod}([M,M])Mod([M,M]) for any chosen nonzero object MMM (Theorems 11.8 and 11.10).
  • VecG\VecGVecG​: indecomposable semisimple module categories are pairs (H,[ψ])(H,[\psi])(H,[ψ]) up to conjugacy; Z2\Z_2Z2​ has two (Theorem 11.11 and Example 11.12).
