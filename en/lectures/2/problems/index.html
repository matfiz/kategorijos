  1. Exercise 1 Universal properties in the divisibility category

    Let Div\catname{Div}Div have objects 0,1,2,…0,1,2,\dots0,1,2,… and exactly one morphism m→nm\to nm→n when mmm divides nnn (so every mmm divides 000, and 000 divides only 000), and none otherwise.

    (i) Show that Div\catname{Div}Div is a category. Find its initial and its terminal object.

    (ii) Show that the product of mmm and nnn is gcd⁡(m,n)\gcd(m,n)gcd(m,n) and the coproduct is lcm⁡(m,n)\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)lcm(m,n), with gcd⁡(m,0)=m\gcd(m,0)=mgcd(m,0)=m and lcm⁡(m,0)=0\operatorname{lcm}(m,0)=0lcm(m,0)=0.

    (iii) Lemma 2.2 says a terminal object is unique up to a unique isomorphism. What are the isomorphisms of Div\catname{Div}Div, and what does the lemma say here? In Set\SetSet, show that the terminal objects are the one-point sets and the only initial object is ∅\emptyset∅; in Vect\VectVect, show that the zero space is both.

    Show a hint

    Divisibility is reflexive and transitive, and every Hom⁡\HomHom set has at most one element, so every diagram commutes. A product of mmm and nnn is a common divisor PPP that every common divisor divides.

    Show the answer

    (i) A category; 111 is initial and 000 terminal. (ii) gcd⁡\gcdgcd is the greatest common divisor in the order of divisibility, lcm⁡\operatorname{lcm}lcm the least common multiple. (iii) Only the identities: m∣nm\mid nm∣n and n∣mn\mid mn∣m force m=nm=nm=n, so a terminal object is unique on the nose. In Set\SetSet the terminal objects are the one-point sets, unique up to a unique bijection, and ∅\emptyset∅ is initial; in Vect\VectVect the zero space is both.

    Show the worked method

    (i) m∣mm\mid mm∣m gives the identities, and m∣nm\mid nm∣n, n∣pn\mid pn∣p give m∣pm\mid pm∣p, the composite. With at most one morphism between two objects, any two morphisms with the same source and target agree, so associativity and the unit laws hold. Since 111 divides every nnn, there is exactly one morphism 1→n1\to n1→n: 111 is initial. Since every nnn divides 000, there is exactly one morphism n→0n\to0n→0: 000 is terminal.

    (ii) A product of mmm and nnn is an object PPP with morphisms P→mP\to mP→m, P→nP\to nP→n, that is P∣mP\mid mP∣m and P∣nP\mid nP∣n, such that every ZZZ with Z∣mZ\mid mZ∣m, Z∣nZ\mid nZ∣n has a morphism Z→PZ\to PZ→P, Z∣PZ\mid PZ∣P (it is then unique, and the triangles commute because every diagram does). So PPP is a common divisor that every common divisor divides. For m,n≥1m,n\ge1m,n≥1, gcd⁡(m,n)\gcd(m,n)gcd(m,n) is such: every common divisor divides it, by Bézout’s identity gcd⁡(m,n)=am+bn\gcd(m,n)=am+bngcd(m,n)=am+bn. If n=0n=0n=0, the common divisors of mmm and 000 are the divisors of mmm, and mmm is the one they all divide: gcd⁡(m,0)=m\gcd(m,0)=mgcd(m,0)=m (and gcd⁡(0,0)=0\gcd(0,0)=0gcd(0,0)=0). Dually, a coproduct CCC has m∣Cm\mid Cm∣C, n∣Cn\mid Cn∣C, and C∣ZC\mid ZC∣Z for every common multiple ZZZ. For m,n≥1m,n\ge1m,n≥1, lcm⁡(m,n)\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)lcm(m,n) divides every common multiple. If n=0n=0n=0, the only common multiple of mmm and 000 is 000, so C=0C=0C=0.

    (iii) A morphism m→nm\to nm→n with an inverse means m∣nm\mid nm∣n and n∣mn\mid mn∣m, so m=nm=nm=n: the only isomorphisms are the identities, and the unique isomorphism of the lemma is an identity. The terminal object is literally unique, and so are products and coproducts. In Set\SetSet, a set TTT is terminal exactly when it has one point: every set has exactly one function to a one-point set, while if T=∅T=\emptysetT=∅ there is no function from {∗}\{\ast\}{∗} to TTT, and if TTT has at least two points there are at least two. Any two one-point sets are isomorphic, by exactly one bijection. A set is initial exactly when it is ∅\emptyset∅: there is one (empty) function from ∅\emptyset∅ to each set, and a nonempty set has no function to ∅\emptyset∅. In Vect\VectVect the zero space is both initial and terminal: a linear map 0→V0\to V0→V must send 000 to 000, and the only map V→0V\to0V→0 is zero.
